# Study furniture source prompts

Final prompts used with the built-in imagegen tool. These describe raster source
art, not runtime instructions or executable extensions. Source images and final
indexed dimensions are linked from the [workshop reference](README.md#study-furniture-artwork).

## Bookcase

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art furniture sprite sheet for TMT Office. Create ONE oak bookcase asset with FOUR upright directional views of that same object in a strict equal 2 by 2 grid, transparent background including gaps. Top left SOUTH/front, top right WEST/left side, bottom left NORTH/back, bottom right EAST/right side. Consistent elevated 3/4 top-down cozy game camera, not isometric diamond. All four sprites have the same world height and baseline, fit comfortably within their own square quadrant with a 10 percent transparent margin, never touch or cross a quadrant boundary. A honey-oak tall freestanding bookcase with three shelves of moss-green, cream, terracotta and dark teal books, tiny brass handles on the bottom cupboard, a small potted trailing plant on top. Front visibly contains books; back is correctly closed wooden planks; sides show the same object's side panels, not four duplicate front views. Rich readable hand-pixelled 16-bit office game art, deliberate crisp pixel clusters, limited warm palette, dark teal brown outlines, mild baked shading only, no cast shadow outside the silhouette. Each view must remain recognizable after nearest-neighbor reduction to 96 by 96 pixels. Truly transparent alpha, no fake checkerboard, no room or floor, no grid lines, no labels, no text, no people, no HUD, no logos, no glow or blur. This is one furniture sprite sheet, not an office concept image.

## Reading lamp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art furniture sprite sheet for TMT Office. Generate ONE elegant freestanding reading lamp with FOUR upright views in a strict equal 2 by 2 grid on genuinely transparent alpha. Top left SOUTH/front, top right WEST/left side, bottom left NORTH/back, bottom right EAST/right side. Elevated 3/4 top-down cozy game camera, not isometric diamond. Same size and baseline in every square quadrant; 12 percent transparent margin on every side. Warm brass slender stand, dark moss-green enameled bell shade, tiny warm cream light under shade, round dark teal weighted base, tasteful retro office design. Rich readable hand-pixelled 16-bit art with deliberate crisp pixel clusters, limited warm palette and dark brown/teal outlines. Maintain the same actual lamp in all four views, physically sensible orientation, no heavy glow or ground shadow. Must remain recognizable downsampled to 64 by 64 pixels per view. No labels, text, room, floor, people, interface, fake checkerboard or grid lines. Transparent gaps between quadrants. Do not rotate the actual sprite sideways or upside down.

## Desktop terminal

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production pixel-art tabletop equipment sprite sheet for TMT Office. ONE compact desktop computer setup with FOUR upright directional views in a strict equal 2 by 2 grid on true transparent alpha. Top left SOUTH/front, top right WEST/left side, bottom left NORTH/back, bottom right EAST/right side. Same elevated 3/4 top-down cozy office game camera and exact same scale in all four square cells. The object is a dark teal slim monitor on a small brass pedestal stand, with compact cream keyboard and a mouse in front, grouped tightly into one sprite. South shows a muted mint screen with simple tiny unreadable pixel lines and an amber status dot; north shows the correctly opaque rear casing and a neatly coiled short cable, not the screen. West/east show physically sensible side views, all equipment stays upright. No desk, no table, no room. Rich readable deliberate crisp hand-pixelled 16-bit clusters, warm restrained colors, gentle baked shading, no glow or floor shadow. Designed for reduction to 48 by 48 source pixels per cell. Keep all equipment wholly inside each cell, consistent baseline, 12 percent transparent margin. Absolutely no words, labels, logos, people, HUD, fake checkerboard or grid. The only visible content is these four views of the same desktop computer setup.
