### What it is

A meeting between you and several agents, run from the terminal. It is part of the [Squad](/extensions/squad) extension, not a separate one. You start it with a topic and the agents you invite, and you decide who speaks.

### Who has the floor

- **Only you start a meeting.** Agents cannot start one or invite each other.
- **You give the floor.** By default you grant it with `g` or by naming the agent with `@name`. An agent that wants to speak raises a hand with a one-line intent and waits; an `@mention` by an agent only queues its hand.
- **Or first come, first served.** `m` switches the mode during the meeting. In this mode no one speaks twice in a row.
- **The floor returns by itself** after the speaker's turn, and a silent speaker loses it to the next hand.

### What it builds on

A meeting is a `tmt room`: its members and its transcript. Giving the floor is a `talk` request to that agent, and its `reply` is its turn, so agents without the floor are not woken and use no quota.

### When it ends

You get the transcript and the list of decisions you recorded. A decision can become an issue or a `tmt talk` to an agent, but only after you confirm it. Nothing in a meeting runs by itself.

### What comes later

The first version is text in the terminal. A web view on top of [Colab](/extensions/colab) and voice come after it.
